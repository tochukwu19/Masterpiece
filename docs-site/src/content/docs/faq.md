## Setup

### The filter drawer is empty. How do I add filters?
Install the free **Search & Discovery** app by Shopify, open **Filters**, and add the filters you want, such as
availability, price, product type or an option like color. They appear on collection and search pages. If
**Enable filtering** is off in the **Product grid** or **Search results** section, the filter button is hidden.

### Why don't my colors show as swatches?
Swatches come from your product options. Edit the product's option, for example **Color**, and give each value
a swatch color or image, or connect the option to a category metafield. Values without a swatch show as buttons.

### Why doesn't pickup availability show on my product page?
The **Pickup availability** block needs local pickup turned on for at least one location with a full address,
in **Settings > Shipping and delivery > Local pickup**, and the selected variant must be stocked there.

### The account menu shows links I didn't add. How do I change them?
Create a menu with the handle `customer-account-main-menu` in **Online Store > Navigation**, or choose another
menu in the header's **Customer account menu** setting.

### How do I show a mega menu?
Give your main menu items child links (second level) and grandchild links (third level). Then select the
header in the theme editor and set **Desktop menu type** to **Mega menu**.

### How do I add the contact form?
Create a page and choose the **contact** template, or add the **Contact form** section to any page in the theme
editor. Messages are sent to your store's email address.

## Design

### How do I change the colors?
Open **Theme settings > Colors** and edit a color scheme. Every section that uses that scheme updates. To change
the colors of one section, change its **Color scheme** setting.

### How do I change the fonts?
Open **Theme settings > Typography** and pick fonts for headings and body text. Vermeil uses fonts from the
Shopify font library, which load quickly and include bold and italic styles.

### My images are cropped. How do I control what stays visible?
Set a focal point on the image in **Content > Files**. Sections that crop images keep the focal point in view.
Image settings also list the recommended size for each image.

### Can I use a different image on phones?
Yes. The **Hero** section and slideshow slides have a **Mobile image** setting.

### How do I change the text in the footer wordmark?
Select the footer and edit **Wordmark text**. Leave it empty to use your store name, or turn off
**Show wordmark**.

### Can I turn off the animations?
Animations are short and are turned off for customers whose device asks for reduced motion. To turn off image
zoom on hover for everyone, open **Theme settings > Animations**.

## Selling

### Can customers buy from the home page?
Yes. Add the **Featured product** section, or turn on **Show quick add button** in
**Theme settings > Product cards** to add products to the cart from product cards.

### How do I use a cart page instead of the cart drawer?
Open **Theme settings > Cart** and set **Cart type** to **Page**.

### Does Vermeil support subscriptions, discounts and gift cards?
Yes. The cart shows subscription details, automatic and code discounts, and gift card recipient details.
Gift card products show a recipient form on the product page.

### Do I need any apps?
No. Every feature in the demo store is built into the theme. Apps that offer theme blocks can be added to the
product, featured product, blog post, footer, hero, rich text and image with text sections.

## Languages

### How do I change text such as "Add to cart"?
Go to **Online Store > Themes**, select **...** next to Vermeil, and choose **Edit default theme content**.

### Does Vermeil support right-to-left languages?
Yes. When a customer views your store in Arabic, Hebrew or another right-to-left language, the layout mirrors
automatically.

## Code

### Can I edit the theme code?
You can, but code changes aren't covered by support and are replaced when you update the theme. Duplicate the
theme first (**Online Store > Themes > ... > Duplicate**) so you can return to the original.

### How do I update Vermeil?
When a new version is available, Shopify shows it in your theme library. Updating adds a new copy of the theme
with your settings and content; publish it when you're happy with it.
