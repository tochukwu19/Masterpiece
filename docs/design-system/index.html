Finding your ring size

A ring should feel secure while still allowing you to remove it comfortably. Browse rings or read on.

Measure the intended finger

Ask a jeweler to measure your finger with a ring sizer.

  • The ring stays on during normal movement.
  • You can bend your finger comfortably.
  1. Measure in the evening.
  2. Check the chart for the product.

Small differences matter when a ring needs to pass over the knuckle.
