# Pages

Each page type has its own template. Select it from the page selector at the top of the theme editor. The
[settings reference](settings-reference.md) lists every setting mentioned here.

## Header and footer

The header and footer appear on every page except the password page.

**Header** includes the announcement bar, your logo, the main menu, search, the country/region and language
selectors, the account icon and the cart. On small screens the menu opens as a drawer with nested levels.

- **Desktop logo position**: center or left.
- **Desktop menu type**: **Dropdown** shows each menu's links in a list; **Mega menu** shows second-level links
  as columns with their third-level links below.
- **Sticky header**: keep the header visible **Always**, only **On scroll up**, or not at all.
- **Announcement bar**: add an **Announcement** block for each message. With more than one, customers can step
  through them with arrows.

**Footer** is built from blocks: **Menu**, **Text**, **Email signup** and **Social media icons**, plus app
blocks. Below them are the country/region and language selectors, **Follow on Shop**, payment icons, policy
links and a wordmark that spans the full width of the page. Change the wordmark text with **Wordmark text**,
or turn it off with **Show wordmark**.

Social media icons show for the accounts you add in **Theme settings > Social media**.

## Home page

The home page starts with a hero, new arrivals, an editorial split, collections, an image with text, a featured
product, testimonials, a set of promises, the latest blog posts and an email signup. Add, remove and reorder
sections freely.

Sections you can add to any page:

| Section | Use it for |
|---|---|
| Hero | A full-width image with a heading, text and buttons over it. A separate mobile image is optional. |
| Slideshow | Several hero-style slides that customers can swipe through. Turn on **Auto-rotate slides** to rotate them; a pause button appears. |
| Featured collection | Products from one collection as a grid or a carousel. |
| Featured product | One product with its gallery, variant picker and buy buttons, so customers can buy from the home page. |
| Collection list | Collections as image cards, as a grid or a carousel. |
| Editorial split | One or two tall image panels side by side, each with a heading and a button. |
| Image with text | An image beside a heading, text and buttons. |
| Rich text | A heading, text and buttons on their own. |
| Multicolumn | Columns with an optional image, a title, text and a link, for example your promises or services. |
| Testimonials | Customer reviews or press quotes in a carousel. |
| Logo list | Logos of stockists or publications. |
| Video | A video you upload, played with the theme's controls, or a YouTube or Vimeo video. |
| Blog posts | The latest posts from a blog. |
| Email signup | A newsletter signup, optionally over a background image. |
| Contact form | A contact form with an optional phone number field. |
| Custom Liquid | Your own Liquid, HTML or app snippets. |

The hero, slideshow, rich text and image with text sections share the same blocks: **Heading**, **Text**,
**Button** and **Custom Liquid**. A button can be **Primary**, **Secondary** or **Link** style.

## Product page

The **Product information** section is built from blocks. Drag them into any order, remove the ones you don't
need, or add **Text**, **Collapsible row**, **Custom Liquid** and app blocks.

- **Gallery**: images, videos and 3D models. **Desktop layout** stacks media in one column or shows a
  two-column grid. On mobile, customers swipe through media. With **Enable full-screen image view** on,
  selecting an image opens it full screen. When a variant has its own image, selecting the variant shows it.
- **Variant picker**: buttons or a dropdown. Options with color or image values show as swatches. Unavailable
  combinations are marked, and the price, image and buttons update as customers choose.
- **Buy buttons**: add to cart and, with **Show dynamic checkout buttons** on, the customer's preferred
  payment method, such as Shop Pay or Apple Pay. Gift card products show a recipient form.
- **Pickup availability**: shows which locations have the selected variant in stock for pickup.
- **Collapsible row**: for details such as materials, sizing or care. Write the content in the block or use
  **Row content from page** to show a page's content.
- **Complementary products**: products you pair with this one in the Search & Discovery app.

Below the product information, the **Product recommendations** section shows related products, or the
complementary products you pair in Search & Discovery. It's only available on product pages.

With **Enable sticky product information on desktop** on, the product details stay in view while customers
scroll through the gallery.

## Collection page

The **Collection banner** shows the collection title, description and image, beside or behind the text. The
**Product grid** shows the products with filters, sorting and pagination.

- Filters open in a drawer. Customers can choose several values, set a price range and remove filters from the
  pills above the grid. Results update without reloading the page.
- Set up filters in the Search & Discovery app. Turn filtering or sorting off with **Enable filtering** and
  **Enable sorting**.
- Product card options, such as the second image on hover, quick add and badges, are in
  **Theme settings > Product cards**.

## Collections list page

Shows all your collections as image cards. Collections without an image use their first product's image.

## Search

The search page shows products, blog posts and pages, with the same filters and sorting as collections.
Predictive search in the header suggests products, collections, pages and blog posts as customers type. Turn
suggestions off in **Theme settings > Search behavior**.

## Cart

Choose **Drawer** or **Page** in **Theme settings > Cart > Cart type**. Both show discounts, subscription
details, unit prices, a tax and shipping note and dynamic checkout buttons. Turn on **Enable order notes** to
let customers add a note to their order.

## Blog and blog posts

The **Blog** section shows posts as cards, with a tag filter and an optional larger first post
(**Feature the latest post**). Posts without a featured image show as text cards.

A blog post is built from blocks: **Title**, **Featured image**, **Content**, **Tags**, **Share**,
**Custom Liquid** and app blocks. Comments show when the blog allows them, with a message after posting and,
for moderated blogs, a note that the comment is waiting for approval. **Show previous and next post links**
adds links to the neighboring posts.

## Pages and contact

The **Page** section shows the page title and content. **Content width** sets a narrow or wide column. Pages
using the **contact** template add a **Contact form** section below the content.

## Page not found

Shows a message, a search field with suggestions and a link to all products.

## Password page

Shows your logo or store name, your password page message and an email signup. Customers with the password
select **Enter using password** below the signup. Edit the message in **Online Store > Preferences**.

## Gift card

The page customers receive with a gift card. It shows your logo or store name, the amount, balance and expiry,
the code with a copy button, a QR code for in-store use, and an **Add to Apple Wallet** button. Customers can
print it. Preview it in the theme editor from the page selector under **Others > Gift card**.
